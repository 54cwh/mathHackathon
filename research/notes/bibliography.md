# 核心参考文献

1. Zador AM. **A critique of pure learning and what artificial neural networks can learn from animal brains.** Nature Communications (2019).  
   https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-11786-6  
   用途：genomic bottleneck、innate wiring rules。

2. **Complex computation from developmental priors.** Nature Communications (2023).  
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-37980-1  
   用途：developmental encoding / compressed generative representation。

3. **Encoding innate ability through a genomic bottleneck.** PNAS (2024).  
   https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11420173/  
   用途：genome-like compressed network specification。

4. Richter O, Schneidman E. **Building the connectome of a small brain with a simple stochastic developmental generative model.** PNAS 122(47), e2504913122 (2025).  
   https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2504913122  
   用途：cell type、birth time、distance、pruning 等 developmental rules 生成 connectome。

5. Miikkulainen R. **Neuroevolution insights into biological neural computation.** Science 387(6735), eadp7478 (2025).  
   https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adp7478

6. Petrucco L, et al. **Neural dynamics and architecture of the heading direction circuit in zebrafish.** Nature Neuroscience 26, 765–773 (2023).  
   https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-023-01308-5

7. Dunn TW, et al. **Brain-wide mapping of neural activity controlling zebrafish exploratory locomotion.** eLife (2016).  
   https://elifesciences.org/articles/12741

8. Trivedi CA, Bollmann JH. **Visually driven chaining of elementary swim patterns into a goal-directed motor sequence: a virtual reality study of zebrafish prey capture.** Frontiers in Neural Circuits (2013).  
   https://doi.org/10.3389/fncir.2013.00086

9. **Feeding State Modulates Behavioral Choice and Processing of Prey Stimuli in the Zebrafish Tectum.** Neuron (2016).  
   用途：hunger/internal state 与 approach–avoidance。

10. **The Calmodulin-interacting peptide Pcp4a regulates feeding state-dependent behavioral choice in zebrafish.** Neuron (2024).  
    用途：feeding state 与 foraging-related decision。

11. **A thalamus–brainstem attractor network drives history-biased decisions.** Nature (2026).  
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10623-3

12. **Multiomic atlas with functional stratification and developmental dynamics of zebrafish cis-regulatory elements.** Nature Genetics 54, 1037–1050 (2022).  
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-022-01089-w

13. Herrera-Álvarez S, Patton JEJ, Thornton JW. **The structure of an ancient genotype–phenotype map shaped the functional evolution of a protein family.** Nature Ecology & Evolution 9, 1656–1669 (2025).  
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-025-02777-6

14. Dorkenwald S, et al. **Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain.** Nature 634 (2024).  
    https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y

15. Sudarshan TP. **Neural DNA: A Compact Genome for Growing Network Architecture.** (2026).  
    https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19248389

16. Sudarshan TP. **Scaling Neural DNA to GPT-2: 354 Parameters Wire a Language Model.** (2026).  
    https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19390927
